# Try a short dictation

**English** · [简体中文](TEST_READING.zh-CN.md)

Turn off automatic sending. Read any passage below at your normal pace, with a natural pause between sentences. Start with **General dictation**; a context preset is optional. The interface language and the spoken language are independent.

## English

Let's schedule the design review for tomorrow at ten thirty. Please keep the API names unchanged. The new release should be easier to install, but we have not finished testing it yet. Increase the sample size by ten percent, and keep the threshold at zero point five.

## Chinese

我们明天上午十点半开会，先检查测试结果，再整理会议记录。新版本应该更容易安装，但这不代表测试已经全部完成。请把样本数量增加百分之十，阈值保持在零点五。

## Mixed Chinese and English

明天我们讨论 the next release，然后检查 API documentation。Please keep the original numbers and technical terms. 我们已经完成初步测试，但还不能确认所有设备都支持。

## What to check

- Text appears during recording. Later audio can revise earlier draft text.
- Native Qwen mode rechecks audio at pauses and when you stop. Wait for the final result before sending.
- English remains English. Numbers may appear as words or digits.
- Negation and uncertainty remain intact: “not finished” and “还不能确认” must keep their meaning.
- Edit the final text before sending if a name, number, or statement is wrong.

These passages check the workflow; they are not an accuracy benchmark. Speech, microphones, noise, and model choice affect the result.
